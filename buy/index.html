Questions

Good to know

Does it cost anything to work with you as a buyer?

The plan and our first conversation are free. Before we tour homes together, you'll sign a written buyer agreement (now standard practice nationwide). We'll walk you through exactly how compensation works, in plain English, before you sign anything.

I'm not pre-approved yet. Should I still reach out?

Yes. Knowing your real budget is step one, and we can introduce you to local lenders who'll give you straight answers — including VA, FHA, and Florida down-payment assistance options.

I need to sell my current home first. Can you help with both?

That's what the upgrade plan is for. We'll look at buy-first and sell-first options and line up the timing so you're not stuck with two mortgages — or no place to live.

I'm moving from out of state. How do I buy without being there?

Many of our relocation clients tour by live video first, then fly in for a focused weekend of showings. Closings can often be handled remotely too.
