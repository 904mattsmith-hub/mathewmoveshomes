Before we list

The punch list

A tight, high-impact list to reach the top of the range. Three need contractor quotes (we're on those), the rest are cosmetic refreshes that photograph well and cost little.

Swap all outlets & switches

Contractor quote

The existing outlets and light switches are a dated brown that reads old in photos. Every one gets replaced with the modern white devices.

Owner has already purchased the new devices. Action for Mathew: get a contractor's price to install all of them throughout the house.

Replace cracked secondary-bath window

Contractor quote

The secondary bathroom window is cracked. Needs to be replaced before listing so it doesn't become an inspection item.

Action for Mathew: line up a contractor to measure and quote the replacement.

Repair stairstep cracking in the brick

Contractor quote

There's stairstep cracking in the brick façade at the front-left of the home, running down through the mortar joints. This is the biggest-ticket item on the list and a common buyer/inspector red flag, so we want it repaired before we go live — the affected brick gets tuckpointed and re-laid so the crack disappears.

Action for Mathew: have Nate (904-886-3667) look at the crack, confirm it's cosmetic (not active movement), and quote the repair.

Brighten the primary bathroom

Recommended refresh

The primary bath skews heavily brown. Recommended: paint the vanity white and replace the light fixture to lift the whole room for photos and showings.

Unify the lighting to bright white

Recommended refresh

Update the light fixtures / bulbs throughout the house to a consistent bright white so the home reads cohesive and fresh from room to room — pairs with the new primary-bath fixture above.

Patch & sand the walls

Prep & paint

Two wall patches in the primary (master) bathroom need to be completed and sanded, and the middle bedroom has wall patch work to finish as well. Complete, sand smooth, and paint so they disappear before photos.

Add a barn door

Recommended upgrade

Add a barn-style sliding door in the middle bedroom — a low-cost, high-style touch that photographs well.
